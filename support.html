R-MAXXING support

Answers to the usual questions. Still stuck? See the bottom of the page.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. R-MAXXING Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds bonus drip and receipt skins. It is not a subscription. Tips are optional too and unlock nothing.

I bought Pro on another phone, or reinstalled. Where is it?

Open the app, tap ? on the home screen, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my brain, streak and receipts?

Everything is saved on your phone only, with no account. On the old phone, tap ?, then BACKUP CODE, and copy the code somewhere private. On the new phone, paste it into Restore on the same screen. The code includes what you typed for each receipt, so don't post it.

How do I delete my data?

Tap ?, then ERASE EVERYTHING (tap twice), or delete the app. There's nothing stored anywhere else to delete.

There's no sound.

The app follows your iPhone's silent switch. Flip it, and check the ♪ button on the home screen isn't crossed out.

How do I turn the daily nudge on or off?

Tap ?, then pick a time under Daily nudge, or OFF. If nothing arrives, allow notifications for R-MAXXING in your iPhone's Settings.

Is this therapy?

No. It's a comedy app for acting on small things. For health, money, legal or safety decisions it tells you to slow down and ask someone who knows. If you're struggling, in the US you can call or text 988, and anywhere else you can find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Still need help?

Open an issue at github.com/Corpslore/r-maxxing/issues and describe what happened. Please don't include your backup code or anything private.
