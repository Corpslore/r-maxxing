R-MAXXING privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version

R-MAXXING collects nothing. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you type stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your brain score, streak, receipts, the things you typed, your Vault collection and your settings are saved in the app's storage on your phone or computer.
  • The brain dump screen saves nothing at all.
  • Deleting the app, clearing your browser's site data or using "Erase everything" in the app removes all of it. We can't recover it, because we never had it.

Backup codes

A backup code is your saved data turned into one line of text. It is made on your device and only goes where you paste it. It includes what you typed for each receipt, so keep it somewhere private.

Sharing

Share cards and share text are only sent when you tap share, and only to the place you pick. Receipts hide what the thing was unless you choose to show it.

Reminders

In the iPhone app, daily nudges are scheduled on your phone by the phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server to make them happen.

Hosting

The web version is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic request data such as your IP address when your browser loads the page. We don't receive or use those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement. The iPhone app ships with its files built in.

Not medical advice

R-MAXXING is a comedy app for acting on small things. It isn't therapy or medical, legal or financial advice. If you're struggling, in the US call or text 988, or find a helpline anywhere at findahelpline.com.
